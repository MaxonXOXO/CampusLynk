1. Head of Department (HOD) Manual

Overview & Operational Entry Point

The HOD Console is the administrative command post for department setup, batch creation, subject allocation, timetable generation, and quality audit verification.

Start Point

Navigate to /dashboard/hod via sidebar or top navigation.

Menu List & Operations

A. Batch Management

  • Create Admission Batch: Select admission year (e.g. 2026), select Tutor & Mentor from department staff dropdown, and initialize class.
  • Roll Over Batch: Move batch to the next semester (S1 → S2 → ... → S6) when term completes. Auto-promotes active students.
  • Graduate Batch: Archive S6 batches upon graduation. Marks students as *Graduated*.

B. Subject & Staff Allocation

  • Add Subject: Enter subject code, subject title, semester, syllabus revision (REV2021 or REV2026), and subject type (Theory, Practical/Lab, Practicum, Drawing, Health & Physical).
  • Assign Staff: Map primary lecturer(s), demonstrators, or trade instructors to each subject. Supports inter-department staff allocation.

C. Academic Calendar & Timetable

  • Timetable Generator: Define daily period schedules, master timetable grid, and assign hall locations.
  • Academic Calendar: Upload term start/end dates, series exam schedules, and institutional holidays.

D. Compliance & Audits

  • SBTE & NBA Audit Desks: View real-time CO-PO attainment levels, syllabus completion metrics, and missing course file logs.
  • Remedial & Attendance Summaries: Review condonation eligibility (< 75% attendance) and department remedial intervention performance.

2. Lecturer / Academic Staff Manual

Overview & Operational Entry Point

The Lecturer Console manages teaching delivery, virtual classroom sessions, syllabus parsing, assignments, continuous evaluation, series examinations, and CO-PO attainment.

Start Point

Navigate to /dashboard/lecturer via sidebar or main menu.

Menu List & Operations

A. My Batches & Virtual Classrooms

Select an assigned subject card. The system automatically launches the appropriate virtual classroom based on curriculum revision (REV2026 vs REV2021) and subject type:

1. Theory Virtual Classroom (/r26/classroom/theory/{id}):

- Syllabus PDF Upload: Upload official SBTE syllabus PDF for auto-extraction of COs, Module topics, and proposed hours.

- Course Planner: Edit 45/60-hour topic schedule, track actual execution dates, and log teaching modes.

- Formative Assignments: Generate AI-assisted question papers per CO, record 10-mark assignment evaluations.

- Series Exams (Internal 1 & 2): Create question paper schemes, enter student marks, generate marksheets.

- Surveys: Initiate and close Mid-Sem Survey & Course Exit Survey.

2. Practical / Lab Virtual Classroom (/r26/classroom/practical/{id}):

- Experiments List: Upload/Define practical experiments list with CO mapping.

- Batch Division: Assign Lab Batches (Batch A, Batch B).

- Daily Rubric Evaluation: Record experiment performance marks using dynamic rubric criteria (Preparation, Execution, Viva, Record).

- Open-Ended Experiments (OEE) & Series Practical Exam: Record internal practical marks.

3. Practicum Virtual Classroom (/r26/classroom/practicum/{id}) *(Joint Theory + Lab)*:

- Unified management for courses having combined theory and practical components.

- Dual series exam evaluation (Series Theory + Series Practical).

- Self-Learning module split-ups and summary generation.

4. Virtual Drawing Hall (/r26/classroom/drawing/{id}):

- Slot Evaluations: Dynamic rubric mark entry for drawing sheet slots.

- Practical Test & ESE Marks: Evaluate drawing speed, precision, and final end-semester drawing exam scores.

5. Health & Physical Education Virtual Classroom (/r26/classroom/health-physical/{id}):

- PDF Rubric Extraction: Auto-extracts customized dynamic physical assessment headers from uploaded syllabus PDF.

- Continuous Activity Log: Record physical fitness, posture, warm-up, calisthenics, yogic asana, and sports performance scores.

- Fitness Tests (CA1 & CA2): Record standardized 40-mark physical fitness assessments (scaled to 7.5 CIE each).

- ESE Evaluation: Enter End-Semester Examination split-up (Fitness Test: 10, Skill Demo: 15, Viva: 10, Logbook: 5).

B. Course Files (`/course-files`)

  • Auto-compiles all syllabus documents, lesson plans, attendance records, question papers, marksheets, and CO-PO attainment matrices into standardized SBTE/NBA course file formats.

C. Remedial Sessions (`/remedial-sessions`)

  • Schedule remedial classes for slow learners, record remedial attendance, and track re-evaluation mark improvements.

D. Attendance Log (`/staff/attendance-log`)

  • Mark hour-by-hour student attendance for assigned lecture and lab slots.

3. Demonstrator Manual

Overview & Operational Entry Point

Demonstrators assist primary lecturers in managing laboratory workspaces, conducting practical experiment sessions, monitoring student attendance, and recording continuous lab rubric marks.

Start Point

Navigate to /dashboard/demonstrator.

Menu List & Operations

A. Lab Workspaces

  • View assigned laboratory batches and subjects.
  • Click any lab subject to enter the shared Practical Virtual Classroom (/r26/classroom/practical/{id} or /r26/classroom/health-physical/{id}).

B. Practical Session Execution

  • Daily Experiment Mark Entry: Enter continuous evaluation marks for student experiment sheets and practical performance.
  • Logbook Verification: Verify student practical record submissions and assign logbook completion signatures.

C. Log & Attendance

  • Access /staff/attendance-log to mark student attendance for assigned practical lab sessions.

4. Trade Instructor Manual

Overview & Operational Entry Point

Trade Instructors manage trade-specific practical workshops (Fitting, Wiring, Welding, Carpentry, Foundry, Machining) and track trade skill progression.

Start Point

Navigate to /dashboard/tradeinstructor.

Menu List & Operations

A. Trade Workshop Workspaces

  • View assigned trade workshop sections and student batches.
  • Access trade practical assessment rubrics.

B. Job Sheet & Workpiece Evaluation

  • Workpiece Dimensions & Accuracy: Grade student job pieces based on tolerance, finish, and safety adherence.
  • Raw Material & Tool Handling: Record tool safety and workshop discipline marks.

C. Attendance & Practical Records

  • Log daily workshop session attendance and verify trade workshop logbooks.

5. Workshop Superintendent Manual

Overview & Operational Entry Point

The Workshop Superintendent oversees all engineering workshops, equipment availability, safety standards, trade instructor allocations, and central workshop course files.

Start Point

Navigate to /dashboard/workshop.

Menu List & Operations

A. Central Workshop Overview

  • Monitor real-time status across all trade workshops (Fitting, Machine Shop, Smithy, Electrical Wiring, Carpentry).
  • View student turnout, safety compliance, and trade completion rates.

B. Trade Instructor Management

  • Review work allocations and session logs submitted by Trade Instructors.

C. Workshop Consumables & Audits

  • Audit workshop practical records and verify workshop course file submissions for SBTE compliance.

6. Tutor & Mentor Manual

Overview & Operational Entry Point

Tutors manage overall student batch discipline, attendance condonation, academic progress tracking, and parent communication. Mentors conduct personalized 1-on-1 mentoring sessions and maintain mentoring records.

Start Point

Navigate to /dashboard/tutor.

Menu List & Operations

A. Batch Master Management

  • View complete student list for assigned batch with live attendance percentages, CIE totals, and backlogs.
  • Condonation Management: Generate condonation eligibility lists for students with 65%–74% attendance needing medical approval.

B. Parent Onboarding & Communication

  • SMS Onboarding Trigger: Send passwordless parent portal access link via SMS to parent mobile numbers.
  • View parent access logs to verify parent engagement.

C. Mentoring Console (`My Mentoring`)

  • Mentoring Sessions: Conduct scheduled mentoring discussions (Academic, Personal, Career, Discipline).
  • Mentoring Diary: Fill electronic mentoring entries (Family background, SGPA/CGPA history, SWOT analysis, advisor remarks).
  • Print Mentoring Report: Export full PDF Mentoring Records for SBTE/NBA inspection.

7. Student Portal Manual

Overview & Operational Entry Point

Students access real-time academic standing, attendance logs, assignment submissions, series exam marks, timetable, and course exit surveys.

Start Point

Navigate to /dashboard/student or log in at main page.

Menu List & Operations

A. Academic Overview

  • Live overall & subject-wise attendance percentages with low-attendance warnings.
  • Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE) score summaries.

B. Assignments & MCQ Tests

  • View published assignment tasks, upload completed digital assignments, or submit AI quiz responses.
  • Take online MCQ practice & assessment tests (/student/mock-test).

C. Surveys

  • Complete mandatory Mid-Semester Surveys and End-Semester Course Exit Surveys (/student/course-exit-survey).

D. Student Mentoring Profile

  • View personal mentoring records, advisor recommendations, and remedial support schedules.

8. Parent Dashboard Manual

Overview & Operational Entry Point

Provides parents with transparent, passwordless, 24/7 mobile-optimized access to their ward's academic status.

Start Point

Access via SMS link or /parent/login using registered parent mobile number.

Features & Capabilities

  • Live Attendance Meter: View real-time daily session presence, present/absent breakdown per subject.
  • Internal Assessment Scores: View assignment marks, series exam results, and overall CIE progression.
  • Tutor Remarks & Notifications: Read direct remarks posted by the Class Tutor or Mentor.
